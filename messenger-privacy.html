Messenger AI: Privacy & Data Deletion

Updated: 17 October 2026 · Applies to the HB Gadget BD Messenger AI app

HB Gadget BD operates this assistant to answer product questions on our Facebook Page. This page describes the assistant's data handling; orders placed on our website follow the store's separate order process.

Privacy · Data deletion · বাংলায় সংক্ষেপে

What the assistant processes

When you message our Page, Meta can send your message text, a Page-scoped sender identifier, Page identifier and message-event metadata to our webhook. The assistant uses these to understand your question, maintain limited product context and send a reply. We do not request your password, one-time password or banking credentials.

How replies are generated

Our application runs on Cloudflare Pages. Straightforward product questions are answered using our current product catalogue. Other questions may be processed by Cloudflare Workers AI together with relevant catalogue facts. Replies are sent through Meta Messenger. Meta and Cloudflare process information as service providers under their own terms and privacy practices.

Storage and retention

The assistant's own conversation-context store saves only up to three recent product identifiers under a key derived from a hash of the Page and sender identifiers. This context expires 24 hours after its most recent update. It does not save message text, names, phone numbers or raw sender identifiers in that store.

The webhook's application diagnostics record event counts and technical outcomes, rather than message text, raw sender identifiers or access tokens. Meta retains Messenger conversations separately, and infrastructure providers may retain operational request records according to their own policies. Messages remain visible to people authorized to manage our Page.

Your choices

You can stop messaging the assistant, block the Page or contact our team for help. Avoid including sensitive personal information in product questions. We use messages to provide customer support and product answers; our assistant does not sell your messages or use them to create advertising profiles.

Request data deletion

To request deletion of data held by HB Gadget BD for this assistant, send “Delete my assistant data” from the same Messenger account to HB Gadget BD, or contact our team at +880 1816-569237. Clearly say this is a privacy or deletion request so a team member can handle it. We may ask you to confirm which conversation is yours without asking for passwords or one-time codes.

After verifying the request, our team will review and remove assistant data we control where applicable. The limited product context also expires automatically within 24 hours of its last update. Deleting our assistant data does not delete records controlled independently by Meta or Cloudflare, and does not automatically delete separate order records. To manage Messenger copies or service-provider data, use the relevant provider's privacy controls.

বাংলায় সংক্ষেপে

HB Gadget BD Messenger AI আপনার পণ্যসংক্রান্ত প্রশ্নের উত্তর দিতে বার্তা এবং Messenger-এর sender identifier প্রক্রিয়া করে। কিছু প্রশ্ন Cloudflare Workers AI দিয়ে উত্তর করা হয়। আমাদের context store-এ শুধু সাম্প্রতিক সর্বোচ্চ তিনটি product ID রাখা হয়; তা সর্বশেষ আপডেটের ২৪ ঘণ্টা পরে মুছে যায়। এখানে বার্তার লেখা, নাম, ফোন নম্বর বা মূল sender identifier সংরক্ষণ করা হয় না। Meta-তে থাকা কথোপকথন আলাদাভাবে সংরক্ষিত থাকে।
